Privacy

This policy applies to Hermes Google Workspace. Effective October 5, 2026.

Information and use

The application uses Google account information and records authorized by its owner to fulfill the owner's productivity requests. Records may include event titles, times, descriptions, locations, participant details, and related metadata.

Processing and retention

Information needed to fulfill requests may be processed by configured AI and messaging providers. Those providers apply their own privacy and retention policies. Records may remain in the owner's application history, files, and delivered messages until removed. There is no single automatic deletion period.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or intentionally use or supply it for general-purpose AI model training. Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access but does not delete original Google records or previously retained copies. Copies controlled by the operator can be removed on request; third-party deletion is subject to the relevant provider's rules.

This website

This static site does not collect application records or use analytics or tracking scripts. Hosting request information is handled under GitHub's privacy policy.

Questions or deletion requests: Contact. Do not include private records or credentials in a public issue.
