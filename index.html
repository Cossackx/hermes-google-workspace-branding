OWNER-OPERATED PERSONAL INTEGRATION

Hermes Google Workspace

A private-use Google Workspace connection operated by the RAZSOC owner using the Hermes Agent assistant. This is not a Google product or a public sign-up service.

What it does

The Calendar connection lets the owner ask the assistant to retrieve schedules and upcoming events, and make explicitly requested calendar changes. Google permissions are granted through OAuth; the integration does not need the owner's Google password.

The current connection requests Google Calendar access. That permission can technically read, edit, share, or delete accessible calendars and events. The assistant's operating instructions restrict which actions it may take; those instructions do not narrow the Google permission itself. Additional Workspace services require separate owner-directed setup and appropriate consent.

Who can use it

This integration is intended for the owner and accounts the owner explicitly connects. Publishing the OAuth app does not create a hosted assistant service or automatically expose calendar data to visitors.

Data practices and contact

Read the privacy policy before authorizing access, and the terms of use. Contact: razsoc.01@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or private calendar contents by email.
